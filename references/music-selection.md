# Music Direction

## Method

Read the final polished script, identify its emotional arc and pacing, and recommend one coherent direction rather than a playlist. Default to instrumental music. Describe energy, tempo range, texture, instrumentation, and what the music should avoid. Do not choose or download a final track.

## Output format

```markdown
## Music direction
- Unified direction:
- Emotional role:
- Energy / pacing:
- Texture / instrumentation:
- Avoid:
- Default: instrumental

## Search keywords
1. [English] / [中文]
2. [English] / [中文]
3. [English] / [中文]
4. [English] / [中文]
5. [English] / [中文]

## Human selection notes
- Check license and attribution requirements.
- Flag search directions likely to return vocal tracks; prefer instrumental versions or remove/separate vocals.
- Human selects the actual final track and checks that narration remains clear.
```
