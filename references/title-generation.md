# Title Generation

## Method

Use the final polished script, not the source article alone. Generate exactly three English titles with different jobs:

1. curiosity / Expected CTR-led;
2. precise content-match-led;
3. professional insight / point-of-view-led.

Avoid clickbait, claims the script cannot support, vague corporate language, and title variants that differ only by punctuation.

## Output format

```markdown
## Title options
| # | Title | Focus | Expected CTR | Content Match | Overall Score |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | ... | Curiosity | High / Medium-High / Medium | /10 | /10 |
| 2 | ... | Content match | High / Medium-High / Medium | /10 | /10 |
| 3 | ... | Professional insight | High / Medium-High / Medium | /10 | /10 |

## Scoring notes
Explain each score in one concise sentence. Expected CTR is an editorial estimate, not a performance prediction.

HUMAN CHECKPOINT — Select ONE final title.
```

The human owns the final title choice. Do not infer actual CTR without platform data.
